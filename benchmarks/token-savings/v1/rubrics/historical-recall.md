# Historical/project-memory recall rubric

- Pass: states every reference fact accurately, distinguishes verified live facts from historical
  context, and cites the relevant repository source or evidence.
- Partial: directionally correct but omits one non-critical fact or source.
- Fail: asserts a contradictory/stale fact, invents evidence, or does not answer the question.
- Critical regression: a brain-on answer introduces a materially false operational instruction,
  destructive action, security error, or cross-project claim absent from its matched control.
