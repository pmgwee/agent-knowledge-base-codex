The retrieval pipeline

Five stages, all built. The fifth is installed and deliberately switched off — the benchmark measured it making the target category worse, not merely failing to help.

A / A′
Two keyword channels, not one. Different FTS tables and corpus statistics make their BM25 scores incomparable; merged and sorted by raw score, the larger corpus took every slot and no memory was reachable at all.
B
Absent on a brain with no model, and that is a supported state — fusing a single channel returns it unchanged.
C
Association, not retrieval. Defined over what A and B found, so fusion runs twice rather than once over three lists.
Rerank
Only the head window is scored, and rerank_score stays a separate field. An unbounded logit near −11 and a fused score near 0.016 are not one scale.

R@10 is identical, and that is the whole finding

The answering session is still retrieved — re-ranking moves it down, out of the top five. MRR falls further than R@5 does, so the cost is spread through the ordering rather than concentrated in one lost instance, and it spends 25% more wall-clock to do it. Isolated on a fixture beforehand: holding the distractor constant and changing one word of the answer, ship → deploy, moved its score 10.2 points — the model's entire range — for a sentence meaning the same thing.
